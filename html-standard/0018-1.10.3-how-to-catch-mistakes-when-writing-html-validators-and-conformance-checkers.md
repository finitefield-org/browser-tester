# 1.10.3 How to catch mistakes when writing HTML: validators and conformance checkers

[Source](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/#how-to-catch-mistakes-when-writing-html:-validators-and-conformance-checkers)

This section is non-normative.

Authors are encouraged to make use of conformance checkers (also known as validators) to
catch common mistakes. The WHATWG maintains a list of such tools at: https://whatwg.org/validator/
