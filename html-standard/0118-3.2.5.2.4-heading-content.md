# 3.2.5.2.4 Heading content

[Source](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/#heading-content)

Heading content defines the heading of a section (whether explicitly marked
up using sectioning content elements, or implied by the heading content itself).

• h1
• h2
• h3
• h4
• h5
• h6
• hgroup (if it has a descendant h1 to h6 element)
